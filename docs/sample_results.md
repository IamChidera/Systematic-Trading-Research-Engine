## Regime Research

The strongest filters were not only moving-average based. Momentum, drawdown control, and volume confirmation also helped identify stronger market states.

Examples:

- BTC 30-day return above zero
- BTC drawdown from 180-day high better than -25%
- BTC volume above 30-day average
- SOL 30-day momentum positive

## Interpretation

The key result was not a single optimized strategy. The stronger finding was that multiple strategy types can be evaluated, compared, and combined within one consistent research framework.
